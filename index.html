Built for Real Financial Moments

fi-fore helps you tackle the jobs that matter most

When you're paid and need to know if you're on track...

See your actual spending next to your forecast. Match transactions to your budget, spot variances as soon as they post, and stay ahead without manual calculations.

When statements arrive and reconciliation feels like homework...

Link this week's transactions to your plan in a few minutes. fi-fore suggests matches between your forecast and what actually posted, scored by amount, category and description, and you accept them one at a time or accept the confident ones together. Hierarchical categories keep everything organized.

When you need to understand where the money actually goes...

Drill into spending patterns across days, months, and years. Track budget rules (like 50/30/20), analyze credit spending separately, and identify trends before they become problems.

When organizing finances across multiple accounts...

See all your accounts—checking, credit, investments—in one view. Project balances forward, track statement reconciliations, and maintain a complete financial picture.

When importing bank data and dreading duplicate cleanup...

Import CSV files; transactions already stored are flagged as duplicates in the preview. Preview and check rows before import, and categorize repeat merchants with rules you create.

When privacy matters more than convenience...

In the desktop app, your data lives in a database on your own computer, and fi-fore keeps no copy on its servers. fi-fore never sees your bank login. Bank sync goes through SimpleFIN Bridge, a separate service you sign up for and pay yourself, which handles the bank connection and delivers transactions to your own database. The desktop app has no subscription.

Who can read your money, and for how long

Where it runs Who can read it For how long
Desktop / self-host Can't read it. Only you, on hardware you own. No fi-fore account, no usage telemetry, no AI vendor in the path. As long as you keep the file. There is nothing to delete at fi-fore, because there is no copy.
Hosted (not available yet) Won't read it. We run the server, so we could; policy and controls stop us, not math. Until you delete the household, and no longer.

Desktop and self-host cost some convenience: you run it and back it up. Hosted trades some privacy for convenience: we run it.

Bank sync goes through SimpleFIN Bridge with your own token, and you pay SimpleFIN directly. SimpleFIN sees your transactions; fi-fore never sees your bank login. CSV import has no third party at all.

The desktop app checks fi-fore’s public release page for a newer version. That request carries no household data and no install ID, and you can turn it off in Setup.
